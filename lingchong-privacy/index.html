灵宠 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 2 日 · 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 2 日

灵宠（以下简称“本应用”）由个人开发者 Yurong Huang 开发。本应用不设账号系统，不为广告或分析目的收集你的个人信息。

我们处理的信息

  • 本地数据：宠物形象、名字、性格、聊天记录、你填写的 API Key 等，只保存在你的设备上（系统安全存储与 App 沙盒），开发者无法访问。
  • 聊天内容与图片：仅当你主动聊天或发送图片时，内容会直接发送到你自己配置的第三方 AI 服务（如 DeepSeek、Kimi、MiniMax 等）。开发者不经过、不存储这些内容，第三方如何处理适用其各自的隐私政策。
  • 麦克风与语音识别：仅在你按住说话时使用。语音转文字由 iOS 系统语音识别完成，可能需要联网并经过 Apple 处理。
  • 相册与相机：仅在你主动选择或拍摄图片时访问。
  • 语音合成：默认使用系统语音在本地朗读；如果你配置了云端语音合成服务，文字会发送到你自行填写的服务商。
  • 内购：购买与订阅由 Apple 处理，开发者不会获取你的支付信息。

我们不做的事情

  • 不收集位置信息
  • 不投放广告，不接入第三方分析或追踪 SDK
  • 不出售或共享你的个人信息

数据保存与删除

聊天记录保存在本机，你可以随时在「设置 → 清空聊天记录」删除；API 配置可通过「设置 → 聊天模型 API → 清除配置」删除。卸载应用会删除本机数据。删除后开发者无法恢复。

儿童隐私

本应用不面向未满 14 周岁的儿童。如果监护人发现儿童在未经同意的情况下使用本应用，请联系我们，我们会协助处理。

第三方服务

本应用允许你自行接入第三方 AI 与语音合成服务。是否使用、使用哪一家，完全由你决定。相关数据处理受对应服务商的隐私政策约束。

政策更新

我们可能更新本隐私政策，更新后会随新版本发布，最新版本始终可在本页面查看。

LingChong Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

LingChong (“the App”) is developed by an individual developer, Yurong Huang. The App has no account system and does not collect personal information for advertising or analytics.

Information We Process

  • Local data: your pet's appearance, name, personality, chat history, and any API keys you enter are stored only on your device (secure storage and the app sandbox). The developer cannot access them.
  • Chat content and images: only when you actively chat or send an image, the content is sent directly to the third-party AI service you configure (e.g., DeepSeek, Kimi, MiniMax). The developer does not receive or store this content; the provider's own privacy policy applies.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: used only while you hold to talk. Speech-to-text is performed by iOS system speech recognition and may require network access and processing by Apple.
  • Photos and camera: accessed only when you choose or take an image yourself.
  • Speech synthesis: system voices are generated on-device by default. If you configure a cloud text-to-speech provider, text is sent to the provider you entered.
  • In-app purchases: payments and subscriptions are processed by Apple. The developer never receives your payment information.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not show ads and do not integrate third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.

Data Retention and Deletion

Chat history is stored locally. You can delete it anytime via Settings → Clear chat history, and remove API configuration via Settings → Chat model API → Remove configuration. Uninstalling the App deletes local data. Deleted data cannot be recovered by the developer.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 14 years of age. If a guardian believes a child has used the App without consent, please contact us and we will assist.

Third-Party Services

The App lets you connect your own third-party AI and text-to-speech services. Whether and which services to use is entirely your choice. Their data practices are governed by their respective privacy policies.

Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy. Updates will be published with new app releases, and the latest version will always be available on this page.
